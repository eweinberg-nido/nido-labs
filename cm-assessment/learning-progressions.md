



**GEMINI-ASSIST**

## Using this scale

Levels are recorded numerically. `1.0` was previously labelled Basic, `2.0` Foundational,
`3.0` Target and `4.0` Distinguished (Mastery). The descriptors below are unchanged.

Each sub-skill is assessed on its own. A student's level in one sub-skill does not depend on
their level in any other, and there is no requirement for a student's levels to be even
across a domain.

### Half points

Scores may be recorded in half-point increments. A half point describes a student who meets
one level consistently and has begun to show the next, but does not yet do so across the
range of their work.

- A student with a **1.5** can do what 1.0 describes without support, and can do some of what
  2.0 describes when a teacher provides the structure or the prompt.
- A student with a **2.5** can do what 2.0 describes consistently, and can do some of what 3.0
  describes, usually in one piece of work rather than throughout.
- A student with a **3.5** can do what 3.0 describes consistently, and can do some of what 4.0
  describes, often in a single strand of a project rather than across the whole of it.

A half point records partial attainment of the next level. It is not awarded for effort, for
the amount of work submitted, or for improvement on its own.

Where a student has not yet produced evidence for a sub-skill, no level is recorded and the
portfolio shows that the evidence is not there yet.
