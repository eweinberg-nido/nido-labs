What this is

Every high school course, and what we found posted in its Schoology materials as of 29 October 2026. It is offered to open a conversation among colleagues, not to settle one.

Two limits worth stating up front. Everything here is read from the title of a document, never from opening it. A title is enough to see what a course calls this document; it is nowhere near enough to say whether the document serves a student well. And where a course shows nothing, the document may simply live somewhere we did not look — a shared drive, a class website, a folder Schoology will not open to us.

What the catalogue looks like

99 of our 161 courses have a document posted; 58 do not. Between them, the courses that posted something call it 6 different things — syllabus, course overview, course description, course outline, programa and welcome letter.

That range is not carelessness. We have never said as a faculty what a syllabus is for or what belongs in one, so colleagues have each answered a reasonable question in their own reasonable way, and several have written genuinely useful documents that simply answer different questions. A daily agenda tells a student what happens on Tuesday. An IB subject guide describes what the programme covers anywhere in the world. A course overview introduces the year. Each is real work; they are not interchangeable.

The question this page is for

The 58 courses with nothing posted are the straightforward part. The harder and more interesting question is the one underneath: what do we want this document to do — for a student choosing courses, for a family trying to understand the year, for a colleague who teaches the course next? Once we can answer that together, asking for it becomes a much simpler and fairer request.
